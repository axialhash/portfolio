Principles

How I think

The beliefs that shape everything I build.

Zero-Training UX

If it needs a manual, it's broken. Design for the person who's never seen software before.

Offline-First

The internet is a feature, not a requirement. Build for the world as it is, not as we wish it were.

Ship, Then Iterate

"Holy shit, that's done." — the only acceptable level of completion. Plans are useless. Shipping is everything.

Indigenous Innovation

Build for your context, not someone else's. The best solutions come from understanding the actual problem.
